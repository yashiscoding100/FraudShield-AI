# Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfers Act) Standard Operating Procedure
Document ID: POL-REGE-2024-V2
Regulatory Authority: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (12 CFR Part 1005)

## 1. Unauthorized Transaction Investigation Windows
- **Cardholder Notification Window:** Customers must report unauthorized debit or electronic fund transfers within 60 calendar days of transmittal of the periodic account statement.
- **Provisional Credit Requirement:** Financial institutions have 10 business days from notice to investigate. If the investigation cannot be concluded within 10 days, the institution must grant provisional credit for the full disputed amount (minus up to a $50 statutory consumer liability tier).
- **Final Determination Period:** Up to 45 calendar days are granted to resolve complex disputes, or 90 days for point-of-sale debit card or international transfers.

## 2. Burden of Proof & Merchant Evidence
- The financial institution bears the burden of demonstrating that an electronic fund transfer was authorized.
- Evidence required includes: device fingerprint match, 3D Secure 2.0 cryptographic authentication payload, OTP SMS delivery receipt, or physical POS biometric/PIN verification.
