# Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) & Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance Policy
Document ID: POL-AML-2024-V3
Classification: Internal Compliance Standard

## 1. Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Mandatory Filing Requirements
Under federal regulations (31 CFR § 1020.320), a financial institution must file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN no later than 30 calendar days after the date of initial detection:
- **Mandatory Threshold for Unknown Suspects:** Transactions aggregating $5,000 or more when the institution cannot identify a suspect.
- **Mandatory Threshold for Known Insider / Account Suspects:** Transactions aggregating $2,000 or more involving funds derived from illegal activity or structured to evade reporting requirements.
- **Account Rapid Draining (Smurfing / Bust-out):** Immediate escalation is required if an account liquidates more than 80% of its stored balance via wire transfer within 24 hours of unusual credential modifications.

## 2. Immediate Restrictive Actions
- When transaction risk exceeds the 85/100 threshold, institutions must place a temporary administrative hold on outbound ACH and SWIFT wire transfers.
- The compliance department must document all associated IP addresses, device identifiers, and geo-coordinates in the FinCEN Form 111 filing package.
